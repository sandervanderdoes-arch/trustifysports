The Regulatory Challenge Facing Football

Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 names professional football clubs and football agents as obliged entities. Clubs are obliged for transactions with investors, with sponsors and advertisers, with agents and other intermediaries, and in player transfers. Every football agent is an obliged entity regardless of size.
